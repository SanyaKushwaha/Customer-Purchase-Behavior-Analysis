📊 Customer Purchase Behavior and Shipping Analysis | Interactive Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Customer Purchase Behavior and
Shipping Analysis Dashboard to explore purchase revenue, product
category performance, customer demographics, ratings, payment methods,
and geographic trends.

The dashboard brings key business KPIs and visualizations together in
one place. Interactive filters allow users to explore the results by
category, season, date, location, and gender.

🎯 Business Objective
The main objective of this project is to analyze customer purchase
behavior and revenue performance across:
- Product categories and top-performing products
- Monthly revenue trends
- State-wise revenue and average customer ratings
- Customer distribution by gender and size
- Revenue by payment method
- Revenue across customer age groups
- Seasonal and date-based purchasing patterns
The dashboard is designed to make these areas easier to explore and
compare through interactive visualizations.

📈 Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard contains several visualizations to explore different
aspects of customer purchasing behavior.
1. Revenue by Top 5 Products
Compares revenue across the top five products displayed in the
dashboard, helping identify the products contributing to purchase
revenue.
2. Total Revenue by Month
Displays monthly revenue trends to help explore changes in revenue over
time.
3. Revenue by State
Uses a geographic map to show how revenue is distributed across states.
4. Count of Customers by Gender
Shows the customer distribution by gender using a doughnut chart.
5. Average Rating by State
Displays average customer ratings across states, making it possible to
compare customer ratings geographically.
6. Count of Customers by Size
Compares customer counts across the available size categories.
7. Revenue by Payment Method
Compares revenue across the payment methods included in the dashboard,
such as Credit Card, Venmo, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Cash, and Debit Card.
8. Revenue Category by Age
Visualizes revenue across customer age groups to explore how revenue
varies by age.

🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters
The dashboard includes the following filters:
- Category: Filter the dashboard by product category.
- Season: Explore results by season.
- Date: Select a date range for the analysis.
- Location: Filter results by location.
- Gender: Explore customer data by gender.
These filters help users interact with the dashboard and examine the
analysis from different perspectives.
